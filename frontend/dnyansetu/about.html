About Institute

Who We Are & What We Stand For

DnyanSetu Education & IT Institute India Pvt. Ltd. is a leading institute located in Nagpur, offering computer courses, education, technical courses, beauty parlour courses and fashion designing courses.

We focus on delivering practical knowledge and industry-based learning, helping students and professionals achieve career success. Our institute is committed to making quality education accessible to every aspiring individual.

Officially incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs,Government of India (CIN: U85490PN2026PTC252150) , and registered as a Micro Enterprise under MSME (UDYAM-MH-26-1090854). We are proud to hold the ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System certification, recognizing our commitment to excellence in Education, Training, Skill Development and IT Services. Our establishment is also duly registered under the Maharashtra Shops & Establishments Act, 2017, ensuring full legal compliance and transparency in all our operations.
